# Checklist: Multi-day camp (Overnight)

!!! tip "Utilize Existing Resources?"
    Depending on what's available at your institution, you may be able to take advantage of an existing multi-day camp to vastly simplify logistics. This could take the form of putting together your own part of the overall curriculumn, or providing computational and teaching support for an existing one.

    ??? "Real World Example: University of Wisconsin-Eau Claire"

        The Blugold Center for High-Performance Computing (BCHPC) at the University of Wisconsin-Eau Claire was interested in developing its own overnight summer camp for high school-aged youth. Another group on campus just finished holding their first healthcare and STEM-themed camp in summer 2026, and reached out to the BCHPC expressing their interest in incorporating an HPC-themed curriculum track. Through combined efforts, the original healthcare camp was expanded in scope to be more inclusive of non-healthcare topics, and is set to cover all logistics and funding support, where all the BCHPC needs to do is develop content for a week-long summer camp in 2027.
