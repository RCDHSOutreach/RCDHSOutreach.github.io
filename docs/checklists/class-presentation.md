# Checklist: Class presentation

Giving a class presentation is a simple and easy way to provide outreach to high school students. It gives them a chance to learn about research computing and data in their own classroom, and is logistically very simple to put together. The primary challenge is finding a local teacher who is interested in having you speak to their class.
