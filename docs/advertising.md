# Advertising

## Overview

If you are running an outreach activity that targets a wider group than just a single classroom, you'll want to make sure that the public is aware that your program is being offered. The scope of how and where you advertise will differ based on what types of students you are interested in targeting, such as only focusing on local youth versus pulling in participants nation-wide for a multi-day overnight camp.
