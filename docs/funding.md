# Funding

## By Program Type

### Start small
??? "Checklist Items"
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Draft a budget by category before committing to an activity type {#START_SMALL_FUN-01}
	**Example**: Categories used by one computing center: off-campus and on-campus activities, food, equipment, consumables such as T-shirts, housing for staff and students, wifi
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Start by finding ways to run the activity with the money and resources you already have {#START_SMALL_FUN-02}
	**Example**: Piggyback on programs already on campus, then collect data and refine
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### List potential funding sources and check each one {#START_SMALL_FUN-03}
	**Example**: Federal grants with broader-impacts requirements, industry partners, local family foundations, alumni gifts, units that already fund outreach
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Ask faculty about adding your activity to their grant's broader impacts {#START_SMALL_FUN-04}
	**Example**: Faculty look for ways to strengthen broader impacts, even a 1 to 2 hour collaboration
	</div>
	---
	
### Half-day workshop
??? "Checklist Items"
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Draft a budget by category before committing to an activity type {#WORKSHOP_HALF_FUN-01}
	**Example**: Categories used by one computing center: off-campus and on-campus activities, food, equipment, consumables such as T-shirts, housing for staff and students, wifi
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Start by finding ways to run the activity with the money and resources you already have {#WORKSHOP_HALF_FUN-02}
	**Example**: Piggyback on programs already on campus, then collect data and refine
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### List potential funding sources and check each one {#WORKSHOP_HALF_FUN-03}
	**Example**: Federal grants with broader-impacts requirements, industry partners, local family foundations, alumni gifts, units that already fund outreach
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Ask faculty about adding your activity to their grant's broader impacts {#WORKSHOP_HALF_FUN-04}
	**Example**: Faculty look for ways to strengthen broader impacts, even a 1 to 2 hour collaboration
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Check with the development office and sponsored projects office before approaching donors or funders {#WORKSHOP_HALF_FUN-05}
	**Example**: Development offices may want to manage relationships with foundations and donors
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Check industry partners' giving or community pages to see what they already support {#WORKSHOP_HALF_FUN-06}
	**Example**: Look at a company's community or giving page for available grants
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Decide whether to charge a fee, and plan scholarships if you do {#WORKSHOP_HALF_FUN-07}
	**Example**: One university camp charges $150 per camp with needs-based scholarships; another camp's fee was $750 per week, raised to $900, with about half of students on scholarship
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Consider a cost-recovery fee for items such as lunch and operating costs {#WORKSHOP_HALF_FUN-08}
	**Example**: Example: a full-day workshop with lunch may carry a fee to cover lunch and operational costs
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Decide how staff, teachers, and student workers will be paid, if at all {#WORKSHOP_HALF_FUN-09}
	**Example**: Pay chaperones a stipend. Pay staff for meeting and camp hours. Consider if faculty and staff could count the activity as part of their job.
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Consider funding or compensating K-12 teachers who contribute {#WORKSHOP_HALF_FUN-10}
	**Example**: A district contact said a university is more successful if it can fund or compensate teachers for their work
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Outline a way to track costs for later reporting {#WORKSHOP_HALF_FUN-12}
	**Example**: Tracked costs can help show funders what the activity needs
	</div>
	---
