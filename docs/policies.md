# Policies

## By Program Type

### Start small
??? "Checklist Items"
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Contact your youth protection office early {#START_SMALL_POL-01}
	**Example**: One university requires registering youth events at least 60 days before the start; check your campus's lead time
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Search with these terms: youth protection, minors on campus, university programs with minors {#START_SMALL_POL-02}
	**Example**: Exact office names may vary by campus. If you cannot find an office, try the risk management office, HEPNet, or NCMEC. These sources have best practices for working with youth
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Read your campus minors on campus policy and forms {#START_SMALL_POL-03}
	**Example**: Behavioral forms and training are often available from the office
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Check IRB requirements {#START_SMALL_POL-04}
	**Example**: Camps themselves are usually not research; IRB may apply if you collect demographics, quotes, or survey data, or want to publish results
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Get parental permission before the event {#START_SMALL_POL-06}
	**Example**: Permission slips, medical release, health form, and who may pick up students
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Complete compliance training for event staff {#START_SMALL_POL-09}
	**Example**: Mandated reporter, minors on campus, and similar trainings; one university requires training every two years
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Run background checks for staff with significant access to youth {#START_SMALL_POL-10}
	**Example**: One university's programs pay for checks, and only certain people need them
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Plan enough time for background checks and training {#START_SMALL_POL-17}
	**Example**: A common mistake is not giving enough time
	</div>
	---
