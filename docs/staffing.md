# Staffing

## By Program Type

### Start small
??? "Checklist Items"
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Name one lead organizer and one point of contact {#START_SMALL_STF-01}
	**Example**: A single contact for youth protection matters, and a single person for parent communication
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Define roles: instructors, helpers, chaperones, logistics support {#START_SMALL_STF-02}
	**Example**: One computing center co-teaches with a supervisor and brings in staff and grad students as guest teachers
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Ask which staff and volunteers are interested before assigning outreach work {#START_SMALL_STF-03}
	**Example**: One director said outreach depends on which staff want to do it, and it is hard to sustain if they leave
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Recruit grad students, undergraduates, and other campus staff as helpers or guest speakers {#START_SMALL_STF-04}
	**Example**: Grad students may relate well to high schoolers; undergraduates can be camp mentors
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Plan for at least two trained staff at all times {#START_SMALL_STF-06}
	**Example**: One university's youth protection office recommends two trained people on staff at all times, and two when taking youth off campus
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Have a plan B for staffing surprises {#START_SMALL_STF-10}
	**Example**: Example: a partner's chaperones changed their role in year 2 or 3
	</div>
	---
