# Partnerships with campus or industry

## By Program Type

### Start small
??? "Checklist Items"
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Ask whether a similar program already exists on campus before starting from scratch {#START_SMALL_PAR-01}
	**Example**: Two computing centers partnered with their university's pre-college and summer programs, which handled admissions, housing, and meals
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Talk to other people running K-12 programs on your campus {#START_SMALL_PAR-02}
	**Example**: One director talked to anyone they knew doing K-12 work on campus
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Identify a district or school contact {#START_SMALL_PAR-04}
	**Example**: One district contact suggested identifying the main contact in the district as step one
	</div>
	---
	
	<div class="checklist-item-details" markdown>
	#### Reach out to local K-12 teachers {#START_SMALL_PAR-05}
	**Example**: Teachers know students well and can promote the program; once they know what you offer, they keep coming back
	</div>
	---
