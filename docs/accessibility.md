# Accessibility

## Overview

Youth of all ages, backgrounds, and abilities benefit from being introduced to research computing and data as a potential career option. While developing your outreach program, it's recommended to make your programs as accessible and approachable as possible as children learn differently whether its visible or not. One way to accomplish this is through the idea of [Universal Design](https://universaldesign.ie/about-universal-design){target="_blank"}, where you make an effort to design everything to be available and understandable to the widest range of youth.
